What I found when researching the convention
  • The four-point sparkle is the most common mark for AI features, but used alone and everywhere it loses meaning; pair it with a text label.
  • Colour: blue to purple to magenta gradients are the habit, and some people now read that look as generic. The warm "Dawn" palette and the single accent are alternatives.
  • Apple Intelligence signals work in progress, not a permanent badge: a glow circles the affected area while it processes and fades when the result is ready (Column 2's running state borrows this).
  • Accessibility: with reduced motion, keep the static border and sparkle and stop only the movement; never rely on animation alone to show that work is happening.
Your decisions, as built into every column
  • Always analyzes the whole folder, every click; no confirmation, no "re-analyze" step.
  • With a filter on, the button is hidden and a line explains why, so it is clear that the filter, not the app, is the reason.
  • Sparkle, gradient and glow belong to the AI button and the running state only; the rest of the inspector stays as it is.
