How should AI culling results be viewed?

Three ways to surface the same data: bucket (Obvious winner / Likely keeper / Needs review / Likely reject), score, burst group and rank, reasons, disagreement, and the per-folder “Wow” ranking. Click a photo or switch tabs; all data below is sample data. Today the results exist but are almost invisible: badges are off by default and the inspector is the only place they appear.

A · Quiet overlay: AI is a layer on the normal grid

Nothing new to learn. A small colored dot on each card (on by default), the inspector AI section carries the detail, and the existing Sort and Filter menus do the rest.

Sort:Filter:
GoodSmallest change; no new screens; works with every existing workflow. Burst frames stay in shoot order, rank shown in the inspector.
CostsYou must open the inspector to learn why. Nothing says “review these 12 first”. Grouping is invisible.

B · Review lane: a bucket bar and stacked bursts

A strip above the grid counts each bucket and acts as the filter. Bursts collapse into one stack showing the AI’s pick on top; expand to compare. Badges carry bucket + score.

GoodAnswers “where do I look first?” at a glance. Bursts stop flooding the grid. Reuses the existing stacks feature.
CostsNew bar to build and keep in sync with filters; stacks and AI grouping must agree on what a burst is.

C · Dedicated AI view: one row per burst, decide in place

A left-rail destination (like Winners). Each row is a burst/group with its ranked frames, the AI pick highlighted and its reason. “Accept pick” marks the winner and rejects nothing else unless you ask.
